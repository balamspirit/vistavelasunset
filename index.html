About the condo

Brand-new, turnkey condo in Vista Vela Sunset, with spacious interiors, an open-concept living and dining area, and a fully equipped kitchen with appliances included. The unit comes fully furnished with designer furniture and high-end appliances, ready to move in or to start generating rental income immediately. Ideal as a primary residence, vacation home, or investment property.

The highlight is its location right in front of the pool, with an amazing view of Cabo’s breathtaking sunsets.

Vista Vela Sunset is a premier luxury development in El Tezal that redefines residential living with a blend of modern design and exceptional comfort. Each unit in this building offers high-quality finishes, spacious interiors, and stunning views that capture the essence of Cabo’s natural beauty. Residents enjoy a fully furnished clubhouse, gym, social area, kids’ playgrounds, a putting green, basketball and soccer fields, and padel and pickleball courts. Each tower has its own private pool, and the clubhouse has the main pool and a kids’ pool. With 24/7 security and a prime location close to beaches, shopping and dining, Building 1 at Vista Vela Sunset epitomizes luxury Cabo living with a scenic backdrop.
